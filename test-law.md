# Test Data Retention and Breach Notification Regulation (TEST-DR-2026)

## Chapter I — General Provisions

### Article 1: Scope
This Regulation applies to all companies processing personal data
of natural persons within the jurisdiction, regardless of company
size or sector, with the exception of sole proprietors with fewer
than 5 employees.

### Article 2: Definitions
For the purposes of this Regulation:
(a) "Personal data" means any information relating to an identified
or identifiable natural person.
(b) "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data.
(c) "Data breach" means a security incident leading to accidental
or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized
disclosure of personal data.

## Chapter II — Data Retention

### Article 5: Retention Period
Companies must retain personal data for no longer than 30 days
after the purpose of collection has been fulfilled, unless a
longer retention period is required by another applicable law.

### Article 6: Deletion Procedure
Upon expiry of the retention period, companies must permanently
delete or anonymize the personal data within 10 business days.

## Chapter III — Breach Notification

### Article 10: Notification to Authority
In the event of a data breach, the company must notify the
competent supervisory authority without undue delay.

### Article 11: Notification to Affected Individuals
Where a data breach is likely to result in a high risk to the
rights of affected individuals, the company must notify those
individuals directly.

## Chapter IV — Penalties

### Article 15: Administrative Fines
Non-compliance with this Regulation may result in administrative
fines of up to 2% of annual global turnover, whichever is higher.

## Chapter V — Final Provisions

### Article 20: Entry Into Force
This Regulation enters into force on the twentieth day following
its publication.
